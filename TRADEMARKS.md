# Locomo trademark policy for OSS and downstream builds

Locomo Music OSS is open source. People may talk about it, teach it, build on
it, and share recognizable OSS builds without asking first. This policy protects
a narrower boundary: it must remain clear which products, binaries, and
statements are official.

The MIT License covers the software. It does not grant trademark rights. A
person who cannot use a Locomo mark still retains every right granted by MIT to
use, modify, sell, and redistribute the code.

## Covered marks

The Locomo marks include the names **Locomo** and **Locomo Music**, plus the
official character face, app icon, wordmark, and combined character/name
lockups. These marks are owned by Immortal Company, Inc.

## Uses allowed without asking

You may:

- truthfully describe software as built with, based on, compatible with, or a
  fork of Locomo;
- call a modified or independently built version **Locomo Music OSS** when
  **Unofficial OSS build** is prominent wherever the binary is offered;
- use the names in documentation, tutorials, reviews, news, screenshots,
  compatibility statements, package descriptions, and factual discussion; and
- state accurately that a project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the
  official product.

These permissions include commercial projects and do not depend on a project's
opinions, business model, technology, or field of use.

## Uses that require written permission

Ask `support@locomomusic.com` before you:

- name a product simply **Locomo** or **Locomo Music** without an equally
  prominent OSS or third-party qualifier;
- use the official app icon, character lockup, or wordmark as the primary
  identity of a third-party product;
- register a company, service, domain, social account, app-store listing, or
  package identity likely to be mistaken for an official Locomo channel; or
- state or imply that Immortal Company, Inc. reviewed, certified, sponsors, or
  endorses a project when that is not true.

## Conditions for OSS-branded builds

A third-party build using **Locomo Music OSS** must identify itself as
unofficial, identify its source version or commit, use its own signing identity,
bundle ID, update feed, and support channel, and accurately disclose its network,
telemetry, and privacy behavior. It must not impersonate an official release or
use the mark to conceal malware or deception.

The `wonderdawg/locomo-music-oss` repository may identify itself as the official
Locomo Music OSS source project. A binary is an official Locomo Music release
only when Immortal Company, Inc. publishes it through an official channel.

If a use falls outside these conditions, permission to use the marks ends unless
the problem is corrected after notice within a reasonable period. Deliberate
impersonation, malware, or an immediate security risk may require use of the
marks to stop immediately. Disagreement with a project, by itself, is not a
violation.

Anyone may remove the Locomo marks, choose another name and artwork, and keep
using the code under MIT. Nothing here limits trademark fair use, nominative
use, commentary, parody, or other rights that apply without permission.
